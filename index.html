Learn Hindi the easy way

Five simple habits that work for children growing up abroad.

1. Hear before you read

Learn the sounds first. Hindi is largely written the way it sounds, so reading comes easily.

2. Link letters to pictures

अ for अनार, आ for आम. A picture makes a letter stick.

3. Speak 10 minutes a day

Short and daily beats long and rare. Say three new words at dinner.

4. Use stories and songs

Rhymes and short stories teach new words without feeling like study.

5. Practise with family

Chat with grandparents on video calls. Real people give real reasons to speak.
